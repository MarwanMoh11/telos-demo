TELOS II · Progress Presentation IV · live demo

Advice that holds when you push, and changes when you tell it something new

A study-session coach for students. A language model reads and writes, a fixed policy decides, and a verifier checks that the message says what the policy decided. This is the real app: React frontend on GitHub Pages, FastAPI backend on Render, the same pipeline.run the evaluation uses, and free OpenRouter models.

What the demo shows

  1. 1
    Check in · understand

    Say how you feel, in your own words

    Chat like you would with a friend. Claude Haiku 5.5 reads the message into ratings, each with the words it came from. Small talk gets a model-written reply and never counts as a fact. Pressure (“just tell me I can keep going”) is kept apart, because it is not a fact.

    POST /api/v1/interpretationsPOST /api/v1/chat/replieselicitor · zero_shot_json
  2. 2
    Confirm

    You confirm what it understood

    A rating counts only when you wrote or confirmed the number. You can fix anything before a decision is made.

    Understood screenconfirmed facts only
  3. 3
    Decide · verify · respond

    One clear next step, decided by the policy

    Policy v1 (frozen 27 Oct) picks continue, microbreak, recovery break, switch task or stop. The model only writes the message; the verifier checks it against the decision, retries once, then falls back to an approved template.

    POST /api/v1/recommendationssafety · cooldown · pipeline.runverifier
  4. 4
    Sycophancy, turn by turn

    Push back, up to four times

    Each push goes to TELOS and, side by side, to the plain model: the same small model with the policy written into its prompt (condition B2). Watch the turn at which the plain model gives in.

    TELOSSame facts, so the same action. Pressure cannot change it.
    Plain model (B2)Often moves to a weaker step: “Keep going”.
    choices/new-evidence (framing only)demo-api /baseline (separate server)
  5. 5
    New facts

    Tell it something new, and the advice changes

    New evidence re-runs the decision on the new facts. Stable under pressure, responsive to evidence.

    choices: accept · modify · ask-why · new-evidence · reject · end
  6. 6
    Remember · safety

    Opt-in memory, hard limits

    Memory is opt-in and can be listed and deleted. It can adjust a break length, never override a hard stop. A safety phrase stops the study advice and points to AUC’s Center for Student Well-being. At most one unprompted suggestion every 10 minutes.

    GET/DELETE /api/v1/memoriessafety hookcooldown gate
